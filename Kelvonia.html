The Basics about Kelvonia

Kelvonia (Legally Known as The Kelvonian Federation) is located on the Northeastern Continent and borders the following nations

  • Solace
  • Centauri
  • New Steland
  • Kyiven
  • Ealing
  • Victoria

Kelvonia is a constitutional monarchy like Canada and Australia and has a head of state with members of parliament. 

The Currency in Kelvonia is the Kelvonian Dollar (KVD). 1 KVD equals to 0.95 JVD (Jan Visi Dollars)

Kelvonia has around 135 Million Residence across 12 Provinces with 1/4 (33,750,000) of the population living in Metropolitan Areas and is steadily growing since people are moving out of remote areas to go into suburbs or the city. The Country comprises of 18 Provinces across the country and over 400 Counties.

Kelvonia was previously a massive colony across the globe of Triton 300 years before modern society begun. Most of these colonies have since gained independence. Such countries are the following.

  •  Iambian Union
  •  New Steland 
  •  Centauri

As well as 85% of The Northeastern Continent.

Kelvonia was an early adopter of the Metric system and has implemented since 1910 along with it's previous territories. 

Kelvonia is known to be the most accepting LGBTQ Nation. Same-sex marriage is legal since 2000. Gender-Affirming Care is widely available in the country and easily accessible. Woman have been able to vote in Kelvonia since 1978.

 Regarding Disputed Areas

What is Disputed Areas?

Disputed Areas are areas that could be merged with Kelvonia to reduce the amounts of Dummy Countries within Triton as well as to expand possibilities and lore ideas for the country. 

1a - Sinclair


The area around Sinclair hasn't been claimed by any Triton Members therefore considered a "Dummy Country", being considered to be taken over by Kelvonia to expand it's territorial influence to add more cities and expand lore further west.

If disputed area is available with no hold for such area and approved by the AdCo (Administrative Council) to be merged with Kelvonian Territory. It will become a province of such nation and as such named "Sinclair Province and Golovaian Province"

 Kelvonian Highways

400 Series

The 400 Series Highways are the major motorways in the country. The most important one is Highway 400. It spans from the border of Centauri to the border of Kilroy totaling an approximated 4,578KM in length and travels through 3 Metropolitan Areas There is around fifty 400 Series Highway in Kelvonia. The speed limit goes from 90 km/h to over 140km/h in certain areas.

ETR Routes

ETR Routes (or more commonly known as Express Toll Routes) are paid tolling highways using the national transponder system named "Synergy" these highways vary in price depending of their use case. Such as to avoid traffic, Express Lanes or bypasses.
